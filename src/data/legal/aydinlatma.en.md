## What data do I process?

- **When you visit the site:** This site uses no cookies and no analytics tools that track visitors. The hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may keep technical connection records such as IP addresses for security and service operation, under its own policy.
- **When you e-mail me:** your name, e-mail address and whatever you share in your message.
- **When you use the contact form:** the name, e-mail address, topic and message you enter reach me as an e-mail through Google's infrastructure (Google Apps Script) and are used only to reply to you. Your message is not stored on the site; only to prevent abuse, a counter linked to your e-mail address is kept by Google for at most 24 hours.

Please do not share detailed information about your health in e-mails or form messages. Such matters are discussed during sessions, under appropriate conditions.

## Purpose and legal basis

Your data is processed to answer your questions, assess appointment requests and communicate with you, on the legal bases in Article 5 of the KVKK of being "directly related to the establishment or performance of a contract" and "the legitimate interest of the data controller".

## Who receives your data?

Your data is never sold or shared for marketing. E-mail is provided by Google (Google Workspace) and site hosting by GitHub. Because these providers may operate servers outside Türkiye, your data may be transferred abroad in accordance with Article 9 of the KVKK. It may be shared with competent authorities where required by law.

## How long is it kept?

E-mail correspondence is kept for as long as needed for its purpose and for any legally required retention period, and is deleted afterwards.

## Your rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK you have the right to learn whether your data is processed, request information about it, learn the purpose of processing and the recipients, request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data, request deletion when the conditions are met, and claim compensation for damage.

Send requests to [merhaba@klinikterapist.com](mailto:merhaba@klinikterapist.com). Requests are answered within 30 days at the latest.

This notice will be updated to cover appointment data when the online booking system goes live.
