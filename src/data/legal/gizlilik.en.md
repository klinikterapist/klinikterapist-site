# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 19 October 2026_

Privacy is the foundation of the therapeutic relationship. This page explains in plain language how klinikterapist.com handles visitor information.

## In short

- **No cookies.** The site uses no cookies or similar tracking technologies, which is why you see no cookie banner.
- **No tracking, only anonymous counts.** No advertising or visitor-tracking analytics tools are used. Anonymous, aggregate counts of visited pages are kept: how many times each page was opened, whether a post was read to the end, which site a visit came from (the domain only, e.g. instagram.com), the device type (phone, tablet or computer), the page language, and how many times the appointment, e-mail or social media links were clicked. No cookies are used; no IP address or anything that identifies a person is recorded. The counts are stored only as totals on Google infrastructure (Google Apps Script and Google Drive).
- **No third-party requests.** Fonts and images are served from this site itself. Opening the site sends no requests to third-party services such as Google Fonts.
- **Only the contact form.** The only form on the site is the one on the Contact page. The name, e-mail address, topic and message you enter reach me as an e-mail through Google's infrastructure (Google Apps Script) and are used only to reply to you. Your message is not stored on the site; only to prevent abuse, a counter linked to your e-mail address is kept by Google for at most 24 hours.

## Appearance preference

The appearance you choose (day/night) is stored only in your own browser and is not sent to us.

## Hosting

The site is published on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical records such as IP addresses for security and service operation under its own [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Links to other sites

Links to my social media accounts, such as Instagram, and to resources I recommend open in a new tab. Those sites' own privacy policies apply.

## E-mail

E-mails you send me are used only to reply to you and to arrange appointments; contact form messages are used only to reply to you. See the [Privacy Notice](/en/privacy-notice/) for details.

## Changes

This page will be updated when new features, such as online booking, are added. The date of the last update is shown at the top.
